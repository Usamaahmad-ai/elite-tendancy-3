The Process

Extraordinarily simple.
Remarkably effective.

A streamlined four-step process engineered to remove every friction point between a landlord's vacant property and a verified, qualified tenant.

01

List Your Property

Register for free and submit your property details. Our team creates a professional listing and immediately begins tenant matching across our verified database.

02

We Pre-Qualify Applicants

Every tenant undergoes income verification, Right to Rent checks, and our proprietary compatibility screening before we ever put them in front of you.

03

Arrange Viewings

We coordinate viewings with pre-qualified applicants. You meet only serious, eligible tenants — not timewasters or those outside your criteria.

04

Pay Only on Completion

Our fee is only triggered upon successful tenancy agreement. No placement, no charge. You carry zero financial risk throughout the entire process.

01

Register & Tell Us Your Needs

Complete a short profile: desired location, budget, move-in date, household size. Takes under 3 minutes. No cost, no commitment.

02

Receive Matched Properties

Our team proactively matches your profile against available properties across your target area and sends you curated options directly.

03

View & Apply

Express interest and we handle all communication with the landlord. No awkward direct negotiations or chasing letting agents.

04

Move In with Confidence

We verify every listing is genuine, every landlord is legitimate, and the property meets legal standards before any tenancy begins.
